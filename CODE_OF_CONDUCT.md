# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Allotment to be a practical, respectful place to evaluate Gardener and
cloud-native operations. Contributors and maintainers are expected to make
collaboration clear, direct, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Use respectful, professional language.
- Focus criticism on the work, design, and evidence.
- Assume cloud costs, credentials, and cleanup risks matter to other users.
- Be clear when you are reporting a security or safety concern.
- Accept maintainer decisions when they are made to protect users or the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Posting secrets, kubeconfigs, access keys, service-account keys, or full tokens.
- Repeated off-topic arguments after maintainers have redirected the discussion.
- Deliberately encouraging unsafe cloud-resource or credential handling.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, or block
participants who violate this code. Enforcement should be proportional to the
behavior and focused on protecting the project and its contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@livewyer.com`. Do not open a
public issue for private safety or security matters.

## Attribution

This code is adapted from common open source code-of-conduct patterns and kept
short so it is easy to apply in day-to-day project work.
